Our Beginning

How We Met

We met by chance at the post office while picking up our packages. One conversation, an exchanged contact, and an undeniable connection turned strangers into friends, and friends into soulmates. That beautiful moment marked the beginning of our forever.

From "Hello" to "Will You Marry Me?"

The Proposal

Oluwatobi's POV

One quiet Sunday in January 2023, while we were doing what we loved most — watching a new action movie on Netflix — he paused our routine to create a moment we'll cherish forever. Holding my hand, he went down on one knee and, with tears in his eyes, shared all the reasons he loved me and why he believed I was his soulmate. Before he could finish, my heart already knew the answer.

I said, "Yes!" And the rest, as they say, is history.

After privately beginning our forever with our introduction and court wedding in May 2023, we're now looking forward to celebrating our love, traditions, and faith with the people who mean the most to us.

Almost There

Countdown to Forever

Every chapter has led us here. The journey has been beautiful, and the best celebration is yet to come. Now, the countdown is on as we prepare to celebrate our traditional and church wedding, surrounded by the love of our family and friends. We can't wait to share this unforgettable chapter of our journey with you.
